Hi all,

This is how I do the USD nostro recon every morning, writing it down before I retire next month. It needs to be done by 10:30 London time because Treasury use it for the liquidity call.

1. Log into the Citrix desktop and open TLM.
2. Check the MT950s have come in from the correspondents. Usually they land by 7am, if not ask Raj in SWIFT ops.
3. Run the NOSTRO_USD_AM match in TLM.
4. Export the unmatched items to the spreadsheet on the S: drive (\\fileshare01\ops\recs\USD_breaks.xlsm) and run the macro.
5. Eyeball the breaks, anything that looks odd over 50k goes to the FO desk.
6. Check the GL balance matches as usual.
7. Email the breaks report to treasury and the desk heads.

If the macro breaks, only Maria knows how to fix it. The login for the old Access DB is user=recsadmin password=Summer2019! (don't change it, the overnight job uses it).
Month-end is different, there are extra steps for the CSA accounts but Dave usually does those.
