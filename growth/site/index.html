How it works

A real marketing week, run by agents.

Not a pile of AI tools. One Marketing Lead runs a weekly rhythm with ten specialists, the way a good marketing team works.

Monday

Plan

Nova sets the week's goal from your targets, your health check and last week's results, then briefs every specialist.

Mon–Fri

Create

Blog posts, SEO fixes, social posts, a creative pack, ads, emails, outreach and review replies, in your brand's voice.

Every draft

Guard

Each draft is scanned for risky claims, banned phrases, missing unsubscribe lines and over-budget spend.

Every draft

Approve

Safe work runs on its own. Risky work waits for the owner, or for the teammate who owns that area.

All week

Publish & capture

Approved work goes out. Every lead is scored A, B or C with reasons and gets a reply drafted at once.

Friday

Learn

Quinn writes the Friday report. Its lessons, and every rejection reason, feed next Monday's plan.

↻ Every week starts from what the last one taught.

Two modes

With a marketing team, or without one.

AUTOPILOT

No marketing team

For founders, shop owners and small companies.

  • The agents run the whole week for you.
  • You only see what is risky: publishing, sending, spending, or anything flagged.
  • Hot leads come straight to you with a reply ready; others get an instant, approved auto-reply.
  • Choose how much runs on its own:
CautiousAnything external needs your yes.
BalancedPosts go out alone if nothing is flagged.
Hands-offOnly spend, outreach and flags ask.
COPILOT

You have a team

For marketing teams that need more output.

  • Every agent works like an extra team member.
  • Each agent's work goes to the person who owns that area: content to your content lead, outreach to sales.
  • Reviewers can approve, edit, request changes, comment or reassign.
  • The owner can see and step into every queue.
  • Leads are routed to your sales owner automatically.

The team

Eleven agents. Every job on a marketing team.

Each agent covers one role and knows your brand: your voice, your customers, your offers, and the claims you must never make.

Content guard

Flags “guaranteed”, “#1”, “risk-free”, health and money promises, your own banned phrases, emails with no unsubscribe line and outreach with no opt-out.

Approval gate

Spending money and one-to-one outreach always need a human. Anything flagged always needs a human. Nothing is published unless it is approved.

Tamper-evident log

Every action by every agent and person is hash-chained, so any later edit to the record is detected.

Dry-run by default

Connectors record exactly what would go out until you switch them on, or send approved work to your own n8n, Make or Zapier flow.

Questions

Fair questions.

Does it replace my marketing team?

If you have no team, Autopilot does the work a small team would do, and you approve the risky parts. If you have a team, Copilot gives each person more hands: the agents draft, your people decide. Relationships, partnerships and PR still need humans.

Will it post things without asking me?

Only what your trust level allows, and never anything flagged. Spending money and one-to-one outreach always need a human yes. In Copilot mode, every external item goes to the teammate who owns it.

Which channels does it publish to?

Today, every connector is dry-run: it records exactly what would go out. The webhook connector is live, so approved work can flow into your own n8n, Make or Zapier automations. Direct WordPress, email, LinkedIn, Meta and Google Business Profile publishing is on the roadmap; each needs the platform's app approval.

Is the dashboard above real?

Yes. The website is built from the product's own code: it runs week 1 for both demo companies with the built-in writers and shows the result. The numbers, drafts, approvals and leads are exactly what the product produces. Only the buttons are switched off.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. It runs with built-in writers so you can try everything. Add your own Anthropic API key when you want Claude to write real copy for your brand; you pay Anthropic directly for what you use.

Is my data private?

It runs on your own machine and stores everything in local files. The health check on this page runs in your browser, so the page you paste never leaves this tab.

Who is it for?

Templates are ready for local businesses, B2B software, financial services and agencies. The financial-services template starts in Cautious mode with banned claims like “guaranteed returns” already set.
